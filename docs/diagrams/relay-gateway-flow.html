What repeats a packet, and what holds it

APRS had the digipeater and the iGate. XPRS keeps both and adds the carrier — the role that holds a packet for a station that is not there. Here is where each one actually runs, which is not where you would guess.

FIG 4 The carrier — what XPRS adds over APRS

APRS loses a message addressed to a station that is not listening. XPRS holds it. This role has no APRS ancestor, and it is the one the app implements fully.
