First release on F-Droid.

* No Google Play Services and no tracking: location comes from Android itself.
* The WebAssembly runtime and every bundled wapp are built from source.
* The Player decodes AAC with Android's Apache-2.0 decoder instead of FDK-AAC.
* The editor's AI assistant is gone; AI can come back as a wapp you choose.
* Everything is now BSD-3-Clause licensed.
