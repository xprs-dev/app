Stations as meeting points: leave a message at a nearby station for someone who is not there; paste their key (npub) in New chat to write to somebody new.
Message info shows where a message is kept. "Repeat" sends one of your messages again at another station.
On a phone the keyboard's Enter adds a line; the button sends.
Web chat: groups show their folders as a list.
Fixes freezes of several seconds on busy networks.
